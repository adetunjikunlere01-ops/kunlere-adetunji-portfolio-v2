Place of Worship · 2021–2022

Religious Center

Academic Project
Academic

A spiritual and communal space conceived around warmth, inclusion and reflection.

Sweeping curves create an inward embrace, drawing people toward a shared center of faith and fellowship. Staggered seating supports visual connection and inclusivity, while courtyards and informal gathering zones encourage community interaction. Filtered light through curved surfaces and clerestories creates a calm, contemplative atmosphere.
